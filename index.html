    What does this do?

    Power Query creates automatic steps names, which often require escape sequences, like #"Changed Type" or #"Filtered Rows 1". This clutters the code, making each step harder to scan and reference.

    PQM Stepper instantly transforms default steps to short, consistent identifiers.
